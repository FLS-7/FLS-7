## About me

I build small-business automations that reduce manual work, prevent operational errors, and make important processes easier to monitor. My current direction is Make-centered workflow engineering: understanding the process first, then connecting the right systems with clear ownership, validation, failure handling, and measurable outcomes.

I am especially interested in workflows around lead intake, CRM updates, bookings, invoices, support operations, reporting, and exception management.

## How I work

I prefer contained, well-documented systems over fragile “magic.” Every production workflow should have a clear owner, defined inputs and outputs, duplicate handling, failure alerts, a manual fallback, and a simple way to measure whether it improved the process.
